Hermes Gmail Reader is a private, personal-use tool operated by a single individual. It lets the owner's personal AI assistant read the owner's own Gmail messages (read-only, gmail.readonly) and send mail from the assistant's own account on the owner's behalf.

It is not offered to the public. Only Google accounts belonging to the operator are used with it.
